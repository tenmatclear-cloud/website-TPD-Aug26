Subject-module tile for the deep-dive days — title, audience pill, period/lessons chips, facilitators and abstract.

```jsx
<ModuleCard
  title="TechEd P5"
  audience="Primary"
  period="AM or PM session"
  lessons="Lessons 1-6"
  facilitators="MIT Sharifa / MIT Philip + HK trainers"
  abstract="To be confirmed."
/>
```

The audience pill is colour-coded automatically: yellow for Primary, blue for Secondary. Sits on the soft blue-white card surface (`--surface-card-soft`). Lay out in a two-column grid for a day's module list.
