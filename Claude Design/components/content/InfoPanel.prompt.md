Titled block of supporting copy — objectives, resources, logistics notes.

```jsx
<InfoPanel variant="accent">
  Develop a deeper understanding of JC GoAI AI learning and teaching resources.
</InfoPanel>

<InfoPanel variant="plain" title="Bring Your Own Device">
  The workshop is interactive. Bring a laptop or tablet, charger, and power bank.
</InfoPanel>
```

`accent` is the green left-border treatment used for learning-objective lists (override colour via `accent`). `plain` is the hairline soft card used for resource / logistics items. A string child renders as muted body copy; pass elements for richer content.
